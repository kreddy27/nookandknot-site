Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Nook And Knot ("we", "us") is a custom gift business based in Hyderabad, India. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us or order from us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to take and deliver your order:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • The delivery address and, where relevant, the recipient's name.
  • Details of the gift you want, such as names, dates or messages to personalise it.
  • Payment details needed to confirm your payment. We do not store card numbers.

Most orders are taken over WhatsApp or phone, so the information you send us reaches us through those channels.

How we use it

  • To prepare, make and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order, such as design approval, payment and delivery updates.
  • To handle questions, changes and complaints.
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We send you promotional messages only if you have asked to receive them, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with those who help us fulfil your order, such as a delivery partner who needs your address and phone number, and with payment services that process your payment. WhatsApp and similar messaging services process the messages you send us under their own terms and privacy policies.

This website

This website is informational. It does not use cookies or tracking tools and does not collect information through forms. If that changes, we will update this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfil the order, handle any follow-up and meet legal and accounting requirements, and then delete or anonymise it.

Your choices

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, subject to any records we must keep by law. To do this, contact us using the details below.
